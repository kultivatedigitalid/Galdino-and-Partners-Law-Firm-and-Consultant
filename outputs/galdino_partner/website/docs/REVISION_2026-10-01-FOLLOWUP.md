# Revision — 1 October 2026 follow-up

This revision supersedes the earlier 1 October photo overlays and single-row directory design.

Main banners share the Home type scale. Home's business-licensing tag and dash are removed. Banner scrims concentrate on the copy, leaving the rest of the photography clearer.

The Services index restores smaller photos with red left/bottom accents. Numbers, captions, gold and black borders are removed. Category and industry exploration links share a quick left-to-right red text reveal and a two-pixel lift, with keyboard focus and reduced-motion support.

Category, specific service and industry detail pages use a split introduction: copy left, a bordered photo right and an anchor CTA. The following context section combines the explanation in two columns with a consultation action. Category directories contain two compact entries per row. Category processes retain the connected-map visual, use specific content and initial-document guidance, and have a black background.

The Industries index has six equal photographic cards, without the numbered sector index. Descriptions occupy at most two lines. The regulatory map explains its role in identifying relevant approvals. Sector pages follow hero, combined context, black requirement map, related services and existing closing content; cases, insights and the duplicate sequence section are removed.

Articles follow Kultivate's compact editorial header, author/date metadata, back link, contents sidebar, reading column, references and related content. Reading durations are removed. Search and filter focus rings are thin red with a small offset.

About Us removes the company identity register, Why Galdino link and experiences section. The founder note is smaller, five principles use a balanced layout, and six photographs form an asymmetric collage. Individual professional profiles start with copy left and portrait right, without a background banner or related service/insight sections.

Contact retains the main navbar and starts directly with context left and form right. The response process is integrated into the introductory copy.

Reference: Kultivate `main`, commit `d8b9448504371a2234fd3c7d6c6431cc1ab95b7f`, verified against GitHub. The user-provided split-banner, two-column copy and spacing screenshots guide composition; their sample text/colors are not used as Galdino content.
