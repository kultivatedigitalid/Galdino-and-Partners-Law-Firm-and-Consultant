# Pricing research — 29 September 2026

Research-based prices are provisional and indexing remains disabled by explicit user direction. All figures estimate a limited professional scope, not approved firm prices, statutory charges, complete certification packages or guaranteed outcomes.

Three provider references are compared where available. Niche services without three public like-for-like quotations are marked inferred. Adjacent references explain the assumption; they must not be represented as three direct quotations. Prices use the lowest credible comparable base where identified, with scope differences disclosed. No absolute market-minimum claim is made.

## Review before launch
Prices are maintained in src/data/service-prices.ts and shared across both languages. Public copy separates professional fees from taxes, government charges, laboratories, technical work, external audits and third-party costs. No structured Offer prices are emitted.

Provider legal descriptions, guarantees and accreditation claims were not adopted. Search-only evidence, bundled scopes and proxy estimates require written confirmation before becoming commercial offers. First review structural advertising, multi-location management, IPPR/IPPT, electrical and environmental SLO, INRIT/median openings, ISO50001 and accreditation support.

Timeline values are provisional estimates for internal preparation after complete data, not authority service-level commitments. All price and scope approvals are P0 launch gates.
