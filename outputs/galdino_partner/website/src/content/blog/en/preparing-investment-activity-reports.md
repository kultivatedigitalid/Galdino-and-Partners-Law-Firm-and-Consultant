LKPM connects investment data with the progress of business activities. Reporting difficulties often start with information being collected too close to the deadline, rather than with the form itself.

## Check the business profile

Do not determine obligations from the company name or the presence of an NIB alone. Review scale, investment status, activities, project stage and applicable requirements. OSS guidance provides reporting pathways, including non-UMK preparation and operating stages.

Confirm periods and deadlines from current official guidance and announcements. A general article is not a compliance calendar for every company.

## Distinguish plans from realisation

Planned investment differs from actual realisation in a reporting period. Prepare source records explaining expenditure, activities, workforce and project progress for the requested fields.

Avoid copying earlier figures without checking changes. Where no realisation has occurred, confirm the reporting treatment against the facts and current instructions; do not insert figures merely to fill a form.

## Build an internal worksheet

- Entity identity and activity/project list.
- Reporting period and activity stage.
- Sources for investment realisation data.
- Workforce and operating progress.
- Issues and clarification notes.
- A person responsible for review before submission.

A worksheet preserves the reasoning and source of figures. Supporting records should remain under restricted access.

## Coordinate before filing

Finance can provide transactions, operations explains facility progress, and management confirms the information submitted. A coordinator helps prevent duplication or inconsistent figures across projects.

Reconcile conflicting records before filing. Reporting assistance cannot replace accurate company information.

## Keep evidence and follow-up records

After filing, retain receipts, status records and evaluation comments. Submission is not a reason to ignore later correction requests. Schedule the next period’s data collection early rather than repeatedly working against deadlines.

When the company structure or activities change, also check whether master records need updating before the next report.
