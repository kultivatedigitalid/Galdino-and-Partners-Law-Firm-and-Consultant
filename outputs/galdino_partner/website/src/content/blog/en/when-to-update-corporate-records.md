Business changes often outpace administrative records. New directors, relocation, additional activities or ownership changes should trigger a review of documents and information in the relevant systems.

## Distinguish change types

Changes to constitutional provisions and changes to company data may follow different administrative routes. AHU guidance distinguishes approval and notification categories. Prepare a change list before asking for a single filing package.

Record the previous position, proposed position, decision basis and intended effective date. This helps the notary or adviser understand the scope.

## Check internal decisions

Confirm who has authority to decide, sign or represent the company. Gather the latest corporate documents and relevant decisions before changing records.

For share transfers or capital changes, simply editing an internal name list is insufficient. Corporate, tax and investment implications may require separate assessment.

## Align systems and actual use

Updating one document does not mean every system has changed. List affected AHU, OSS, tax, banking, contract and sector permit records. Identify which systems depend on output from another.

For example, a new address may affect both company records and operating site data. Distinguish these contexts so no facility is overlooked.

## Prepare a change register

- The information being changed.
- Previous and proposed values.
- Decision basis and supporting records.
- Affected systems and contracts.
- Task owner and update status.
- Completion evidence and final checks.

Use the same register at handover so administration does not depend on scattered conversations.

## After completion

Recheck spelling, addresses, authority, activities and dates in the final output. Archive previous versions orderly and identify the active documents. Inform teams that use the information in contracts or transactions.

An initial consultation can help categorise changes and map the sequence before the company commissions work that may not cover every necessary update.
