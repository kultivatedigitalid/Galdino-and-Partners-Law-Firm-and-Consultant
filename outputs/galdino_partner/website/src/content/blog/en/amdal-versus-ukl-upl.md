AMDAL and UKL-UPL both concern environmental impacts, but the choice is not based on the cheapest package. Activity screening is the starting point for determining the appropriate instrument.

## Understand their roles

AMDAL concerns significant-impact assessment for qualifying proposed activities. UKL-UPL documents management and monitoring efforts for activities in the applicable category. SPPL is another instrument for activities meeting its criteria.

These instruments cannot be exchanged simply because an applicant wants a shorter process. Selection requires the actual activity and relevant rules to be considered.

## Prepare screening information

Record activities, capacity, area, location, processes and supporting facilities. Establish whether the project is new, an expansion or a change to a facility with existing documents.

Site context matters because area conditions can affect assessment. Use coordinates and boundaries consistent with the site plan.

## Connect impacts with controls

Map wastewater, emissions, waste, noise and vehicle movements as relevant. Identify information sources, responsible people and technical-study needs. Do not present estimated figures as measured test results.

Certain technical approvals may form part of the document dependencies. Their applicability depends on the activity and control systems.

## Beyond document preparation

Management and monitoring must be implemented and recorded against commitments. Plan who collects data, how measurements are archived and how follow-up is documented.

An initial consultation should establish missing information and a clear screening pathway before agreeing the preparation scope.
