Issuing a business identification number is an important milestone, but it does not automatically complete every operational obligation. Follow-up requirements depend on risk level, sector, products, location, and later changes.

## Build a compliance calendar

Record each obligation in one register with a task owner, supporting documents, frequency, validity period, and consequences of delay. Review the calendar with operations, finance, and human resources.

## Track changes

Changes to an address, management, shareholders, activities, capacity, or products may require updates to records and licences. Create an internal trigger so the legal team receives information before a change is implemented.

## Keep process evidence

Archive final documents, receipts, material correspondence, and the basis for decisions. Consistent file naming supports internal audits and future renewals.

Continuing obligations must be verified against the business profile. A general checklist should not be the sole basis for compliance.
