# Project Context

## Purpose

This website positions Galdino & Partner as a business licensing consultant, law firm, and legal-regulatory partner in Indonesia. It must help decision-makers understand the offer, assess credibility, read useful regulatory content, and start a consultation.

## Audience

- Indonesian business owners, founders, directors, operations teams, and in-house legal teams.
- Investors and international teams requiring English-language orientation.
- Companies establishing, expanding, regularising, or maintaining business activities in Indonesia.

## Positioning

The firm translates regulatory complexity into ordered, decision-ready steps. The site must never resemble a generic digital agency. Copy should connect legal requirements to business operations without guaranteeing authority-controlled outcomes.

## Current status

Legal name, domain, address, registration, final services, professional roster, official photography, project evidence, testimonials, client logos, privacy policy, and terms remain structured placeholders. `src/data/site.ts` is the central source for business identity, navigation, contact details, service categories, and experience placeholders.

## Approved decisions

Astro 7, TypeScript, Tailwind 4, static-first output, one Vercel serverless contact route, Markdown blog, no CMS, Indonesian and English at launch, restrained micro-animation, no WebGL/3D, and Vercel deployment.
